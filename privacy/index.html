Privacy Policy

Google Drive backup · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Which products this covers

This policy applies to the configuration backup feature of our server consoles, currently:

  • ELIX Remote Access

What the app accesses

Only files the console itself creates in your Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see, open, change or delete any other files in your Drive.

What the app stores

The sign-in token issued by Google is stored only on your own server, so scheduled backups can run. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, sell or share your data. Backup files go directly from your server to your Google Drive.

Removing access

Use "Disconnect Google Drive" in the console, or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
